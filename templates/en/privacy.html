Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025 • ARISTON Wash & Dry

This Privacy Policy explains how ARISTON Wash & Dry collects, stores, and processes users’ personal data, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Data Controller

The data controller is ARISTON Wash & Dry. For any matter related to your personal data, you may contact us through the website’s contact form.

2. Personal Data We Collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Account details (username, encrypted password)
  • Messages submitted via the contact form
  • Basic service usage information (e.g. registration date)

3. Purpose of Data Processing

Your data is used exclusively for:

  • Creating and managing your user account
  • Sending service-related notifications or updates
  • Communicating with you when necessary
  • Improving the overall user experience

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Processing of your data is based on:

  • Your consent during registration or use of the contact form
  • The necessity to provide the requested service
  • The legitimate interest of the business in improving its services

5. Data Retention Period

Your data is retained for as long as your account remains active or as long as necessary to provide the service. In the event of account deletion, your personal data will be permanently deleted in accordance with the service’s data retention policy.

6. User Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (“right to be forgotten”)
  • Restriction of processing
  • Data portability
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant Data Protection Authority

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The use of cookies is described in detail in the Cookies Policy. No advertising or profiling cookies are used without your explicit consent.

8. Data Sharing with Third Parties

Your data is neither sold nor shared with third parties, except where required by law or necessary for the operation of the service (e.g. hosting provider).

By using this service, you accept this Privacy Policy. Any updates will be published on this page.
